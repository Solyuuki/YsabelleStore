## Ownership Rule

M1 owns visible UI cleanup, POS and Sales integration, app-shell consistency, and merge conflict resolution. M2 owns
forecasting foundations, forecast contracts, and reports foundations. M3 owns product and inventory data foundations,
stock movement logic, and the clean data needed for SARIMA.

All three members must coordinate on shared data shapes before implementation starts.
